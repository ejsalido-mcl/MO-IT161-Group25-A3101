Welcome to NetGuard

NetGuard is a simple self-assessment tool that helps you check the security of your home or small-office Wi-Fi setup. Answer a few quick questions about your network, and we'll give you a risk score plus easy steps to improve your security.



How It Works

  1. Answer a short set of questions about your Wi-Fi setup.
  2. NetGuard calculates a risk score based on your answers.
  3. See your result: Low, Medium, or High Risk.
  4. Get a checklist of clear, actionable fixes for anything flagged.

Understanding Your Risk Score

  • Low Risk – Your Wi-Fi setup is fairly secure. Keep it up!
  • Medium Risk – Some settings could be improved. Check the checklist.
  • High Risk – Your network may be vulnerable. Fix the issues as soon as possible.

Quick Security Tips

  • Use a strong, unique Wi-Fi password.
  • Change your router's default admin login.
  • Enable WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.
  • Update your router's firmware regularly.
  • Set up a guest network for visitors.
