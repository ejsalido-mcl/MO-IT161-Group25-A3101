About NetGuard

NetGuard was created to help everyday users understand the security of their Wi-Fi networks. Many people don't realize their home network may be vulnerable to unauthorized access.

This tool asks a few simple questions and gives you a clear risk score with actionable steps to improve your security.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does NetGuard calculate my risk score?

Based on your answers, we check for common security issues like weak passwords, outdated firmware, and missing encryption.

Is my information saved?

Your assessment result (risk level and score) is saved on our server so we can see overall trends. Your individual answers and personal details are not saved with it. Messages you send through the Contact page are saved so our team can reply to you.

What should I do if I get a High Risk score?

Follow the checklist provided. Start with changing your Wi-Fi password and updating your router's firmware.

Ready to check your network?

It takes about two minutes, and you'll get a personalized checklist at the end.
