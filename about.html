About NetGuard

NetGuard was developed to empower everyday users with clear visibility into their Wi-Fi network security. Many homeowners and individuals remain unaware that their home networks may be exposed to unauthorized access and potential security risks.

This tool asks a few simple questions and gives you a clear risk score with actionable steps to improve your security.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does NetGuard calculate my risk score?

NetGuard evaluates your response inputs against industry-standard security criteria, checking for vulnerabilities such as weak authentication credentials, outdated router firmware, and unencrypted network traffic.

Is my data stored or transmitted?

No. NetGuard operates entirely client-side within your web browser. Your inputs are processed locally and are never stored, logged, or transmitted to external servers.

What actions should I take if I receive a High Risk score?

Review the prioritized remediation checklist generated with your results. Immediate recommended actions include updating your Wi-Fi network password to a strong passphrase and installing the latest firmware update for your router.
